SafeContext Privacy Gate false-positive fix

Replace exactly:
  src/safecontext/validate.py
  tests/test_validate.py

Why:
The privacy gate correctly re-detected contextual fields after protection, but
it did not distinguish SafeContext-generated pseudonyms/redaction markers from
raw sensitive values.

The fix treats only canonical SafeContext outputs as already protected:
  [SECRET_REDACTED]
  <KIND>_<8 uppercase hexadecimal characters>

A lookalike such as CUSTOMER_ID_NOTSAFE remains BLOCKED.

Suggested commit:
  fix: recognize protected values in privacy gate
